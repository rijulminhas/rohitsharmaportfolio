- A grid of identical rounded cards for every section.
- Excessive pill-shaped labels and skill badges.
- Unnecessary animations or scrolling effects.
- Fake statistics, invented testimonials, or fictional client logos.
- Generic copy such as "Transforming ideas into digital experiences."
- Decorative elements that do not improve the visual hierarchy or usability.

The design should look considered and believable, as though a designer and developer deliberately built it for this individual.
